Solar: Texas Property Code 202.010(b) says a property owners' association 'may not include or enforce a provision in a dedicatory instrument that prohibits or restricts a property owner from installing a solar energy device', subject to exceptions in 202.010(d)-(f): e.g. devices on HOA/common property; anywhere other than the roof or a fenced yard/patio; roof devices above the roofline, outside an HOA-designated area (unless that cuts output more than 10%), not following the roof slope, or with non-silver/bronze/black frames; yard devices taller than the fence; devices that void warranties; installs without prior HOA approval; and during a developer period in developments of fewer than 51 units.
